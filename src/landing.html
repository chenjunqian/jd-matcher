JD Matcher

AI-Powered Job Matching

Match your resume against fresh remote job postings — run it locally as an AI agent skill, or let the Telegram bot do it for you.

Local Agent Skill Recommended

A self-contained skill for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, pi and other agents. It crawls remote jobs into a local Markdown knowledge base, matches them against your resume, and renders an interactive HTML dashboard.

  • Local-first: no account, no database, your resume never leaves your machine
  • Only postings from the last 2 months that you have not seen before
  • Filtered against talent-agency and freelance-marketplace listings
$ bash scripts/install-skill.sh --scope global

Requires Node 22.6+ or Bun, and a clone of the repo: github.com/chenjunqian/jd-matcher
